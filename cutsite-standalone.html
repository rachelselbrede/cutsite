Ready to edit a genome?

A stretch of DNA lights up beside its PAM, the signal Cas9 cuts by. Snip it before the window closes. Cutting anything else is an off-target edit.

How to play

Click the glowing target as fast as you can. Speed and streaks multiply your score, and the reaction window tightens as you land cuts.

An off-target cut resets the combo and, when Cas9 would have refused the site, jams the blades for a moment.

Guide RNA mode lights up decoys too. Read the guide and cut only the site that matches it beside a real NGG PAM. Sites face either way: a PAM to the left of a glow marks a bottom-strand site, read right to left.

Daily: twelve targets under Guide RNA rules, the same for everyone today. Your first finished run counts; share the result with your lab.

Keyboard works too: arrows move along the strand, J or the digits 1 to 3 jump to a glowing site, Enter cuts, Esc ends a Zen or Daily round.
